How I test

  1. Understand

    Work out what the product is for, who uses it and what failure would cost them.

  2. Plan

    Define scope, risks and entry and exit criteria, then design test cases around the riskiest paths.

  3. Explore

    Compare behaviour against a known-good baseline and follow anything that looks off.

  4. Report & automate

    Write bugs a developer can reproduce first time, then automate stable checks so regressions get caught in CI.
